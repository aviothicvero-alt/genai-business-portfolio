## 🎯 Industry Skill Matrix

This portfolio demonstrates proficiency across three core domains: **Generative AI Techniques**, **Business & Data Analytics**, and **Aviation Management Operations**.

### 1. Generative AI & Prompt Engineering
* **Structured Prompt Design:** Execution of zero-shot, few-shot, role-based, and chain-of-thought prompting methodologies to extract structured outputs.
* **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Understanding grounded context retrieval, embedding concepts, and reducing model hallucinations in domain-specific tasks.
* **Multimodal Generation:** Leveraging AI image generation, automated design, and AI-assisted presentation frameworks for strategic communication.
* **Model Evaluation & Fact Verification:** Critical evaluation of Large Language Model (LLM) outputs using rigorous fact-checking and comparative benchmark metrics.
* **Responsible AI & Governance:** Application of ethical guidelines, human-in-the-loop oversight, data privacy practices, and citation transparency.

### 2. Business Analysis & Problem Solving
* **Process Automation & Productivity:** Mapping business workflows and identifying high-impact AI integration points.
* **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Basic exploratory data manipulation using **Python**, **Pandas**, and **NumPy** within Google Colab environments.
* **Strategic Communication:** Crafting executive summaries, business emails, and analytical reports using AI-driven editorial refinement.
* **Market & Use-Case Evaluation:** Conducting competitive tool assessments (e.g., ChatGPT vs. Google Gemini) to align tool strengths with operational requirements.

### 3. Aviation Management Applications
* **Airport Ground Operations:** Applying AI to model delay communication workflows, terminal resource management, and customer assistance.
* **Airline Customer Experience (CX):** Structuring prompt frameworks for passenger feedback handling, crisis communication, and passenger sentiment analysis.
* **Aviation Logistics & Supply Chain:** Exploring predictive maintenance communication protocols and inventory management automation.
